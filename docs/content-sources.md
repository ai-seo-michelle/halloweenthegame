## Fact Mapping

- Game mode, 1v4 structure, Story Mode: The Night He Came Home, dynamic map features, NPC interactions, and authority-alert survival loop: Steam store, official launch materials, official multiplayer overview.
- Character roster: official legacy character and launch overview posts. Confirmed legacy Heroes: Bob, Lynda, Annie, Laurie. Confirmed new Heroes: Jennifer, Tanya, Rachel, Eric, Marcus, Thomas.
- Launch maps and landmarks: official locations overview plus map-specific posts. Confirmed maps: East Haddonfield, Haddonfield Heights, Orange Grove Estates, Haddonfield Town Center.
- Escape routes: AllThings.How, GamesRadar, Mobalytics/Gamebase, and GamerBlurb align on storm cellar, sedan, gate, and police wagon. Police car escape is mentioned by some current guide coverage but treated as conditional, not guaranteed.
- Police flow: official material supports alerting authorities and NPC interactions; current guide coverage supports phones, resident police commands, response escalation, and wagon extraction.
- Michael tools: official multiplayer/progression posts confirm Killer Sense, Stalk, Shape Jump, Shape Dash, Detection Pulse, Blackout, Reality Tear, Evil Presence, and related ability framing. Patch 1.0.1 confirms early tuning for Stalk, Shape Dash, Reality Tear, Detection Pulse, and Evil Presence.
- Items: route-item usage is drawn from multiple escape guides; Repair Kit spawn behavior is directly supported by official patch 1.0.1 notes. Exact item coordinates are intentionally not stated.
- Story walkthrough: official story-mode material confirms Michael perspective and Smith's Grove setup. Whisper of the House and Destructoid walkthrough coverage support the reported chapter flow used for high-level progression guidance.
